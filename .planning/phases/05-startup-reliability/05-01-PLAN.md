Optimize VSTO add-in startup to stay under Outlook's 1,000 ms resiliency threshold by eliminating CRL check delays, adding high-resolution timing instrumentation, deferring initialization outside the measurement window, and verifying the static constructor isolation of heavy types.

This plan addresses the three main contributors: Authenticode CRL checks (up to 15 seconds on air-gapped networks), potential static constructor chains during startup, and lack of instrumentation to diagnose timing issues.
